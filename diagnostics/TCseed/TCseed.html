TC Seed Convective Organization Diagnostic

This POD calculates convective organization metrics for developing and non-developing tropical cyclone seed disturbances. These metrics include a simple fractional area coverage of rainfall rates at and above the 95th percentile and complex clustering metrics such as Lorg, Iorg, and SCAI. Snapshots are taken along the track of each developing and non-developing track, the metrics are calculated for each snapshot, and then composited according to the maximum wind speed (Vmax bins of 6-9 m/s, 9-12 m/s, and 12-15 m/s). We compare results from "observational data" ERA5 and to the climate model simulation.
